# Code of conduct

Kiled follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and respectful in issues, pull requests and anywhere else you act for
the project. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others'
private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@timwuhaotian](https://github.com/timwuhaotian),
through the contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is protected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what is a violation and responds as the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on
how serious and how repeated the behavior is.
